Privacy policy

Short version: we don't collect personal data, we don't use tracking cookies, and we don't sell anything about you.

What we store

The site runs entirely in your browser. Your searches are not sent to us. We store two small settings in your browser's local storage, only on your device:

  • theme: whether you picked light or dark mode.
  • notice-ok: that you dismissed the privacy notice.

You can clear these anytime by clearing your browser's site data.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled, we use a cookieless, privacy-friendly service that counts page views without identifying you, setting cookies, or tracking you across sites.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of any web request. "Read more" links go to Wikipedia and Google News, and their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
